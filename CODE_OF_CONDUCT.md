# Community conduct

JevArena welcomes constructive contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Critique results and methods, not people. Explain disagreements with reproducible evidence.
- Be respectful. No harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing or unwanted sexual content.
- Do not post secrets, private conversations, personal data or material you lack permission to share.
- Label synthetic data, author-reported observations and independently reproduced results honestly.
- Do not spam or promote unrelated products.

These expectations apply to project Issues, PRs and Discussions. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations; serious or repeated violations can result in a ban. Report concerns privately to richard@learnest.org with a minimal description, not sensitive attachments. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
